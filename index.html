NextCredit — BI & AI platform for credit risk

Business Intelligence · Machine Learning · Explainable AI · RAG assistant

▶ Video demo

Context

A financial institution needs to monitor its credit portfolio, anticipate customer defaults and justify every lending decision. The data (credit applications, payment behavior, customer profiles) was scattered and hard to use.

What I did

  • Designed the data warehouse (star schema: Fait_Demande, Fait_Comportement, Customer, Date, Product, Contract, Channel dimensions…).
  • Built the ETL pipelines with Talend & SQL Server to integrate, clean and historize the data.
  • 4 Power BI dashboards: Overview, Portfolio performance, Customer profile, Arrears & default.
  • Computed regulatory risk indicators: PD, LGD, EAD, Expected Loss, Unexpected Loss, NPL ratio, recovery rate.
  • Machine Learning models to predict default, made transparent with SHAP & LIME.
  • LLM-powered RAG assistant to query indicators and understand decision explanations in natural language.
  • Integrated into a web application (Flask API + Angular front end).

Reducing CO₂ emissions in the agri-food industry

Business Intelligence · Sustainability · Prediction

▶ Video demo

Context

A multi-site agri-food company (Tunis, Sousse, Sfax, Gabès) needs to measure its carbon footprint (Scopes 1, 2 and 3), stay within its emissions quota and identify reduction levers — for decision-makers who are not technical experts.

What I did

  • Collected and processed emissions data with Python & Talend.
  • 6 Power BI pages: Home, Materials, Equipment, Suppliers, Competitors, Quota & prediction.
  • Analysis by Scope 1 / 2 / 3, by site, equipment, raw material and supplier (ISO 14001, organic certifications).
  • Competitive benchmark (Danone, Lactalis, Nestlé): CO₂ impact, water, energy mix, sustainability score.
  • Prediction models: equipment failure risk and environmental impact by category.
  • Recommendation assistant that turns analysis results into concrete actions (waste reduction, energy efficiency).
  • Tracked the impact of implemented actions through the same reporting, aligned with SDGs 7, 9, 12 and 13.

Analytics project

Airbnb Europe — Bookings and revenue analysis

ETL SSIS · Data Warehouse SQL Server · Cube SSAS · Power BI

Analysis of Airbnb performance across major European cities (London, Paris, Rome, Amsterdam, Vienna, Athens, Barcelona, Berlin…): where revenue is concentrated, which listing types perform best, how activity evolved from 2020 to 2023 and what satisfaction looks like per listing.

  • End-to-end BI chain on the Microsoft SQL Server stack: from raw data to dashboard.
  • ETL with SSIS: extraction, cleaning and loading of the data (listings, bookings, hosts, neighbourhoods) into the warehouse.
  • Designed the data warehouse on SQL Server, managed with SSMS (star schema: bookings fact; listing, city, neighbourhood, date, day-type dimensions).
  • Built an SSAS cube for multidimensional analysis, connected to Power BI.
  • DAX measures (revenue, number of bookings, satisfaction ratio).
  • Interactive 2-page dashboard with month / listing type / city filters and button navigation.
  • Geographic map, revenue treemap by city, satisfaction matrix by neighbourhood.
  • Insights: London and Paris = 61% of revenue, a drop in 2022 followed by a strong rebound in 2023, more bookings on weekends.
